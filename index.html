    Japanese test routine

    1. ミラー rear-view + door mirror on the side you are going to.
    2. 合図 signal 30 m before a turn, 3 s before a lane change.
    3. 目視 shoulder check right before you turn / move over.
    • Keep left. Left turns: hug the left. Right turns: go close to the centre line.
    • 止まれ and 踏切: stop with the front bumper just before the line, look left & right (Z then C), then go.
    • Traffic light: never enter on red; amber only if you cannot stop safely.
    • S字 / クランク: slowly (≤ 10 km/h), no wheel over the kerb, don't touch the poles.
    • 坂道: stop on the slope, then move off without rolling back.
    • Outer straight: accelerate to about 40 km/h, never above 40.
    • Finish at 発着点: signal left, pull in within 30 cm of the kerb, stop at the mark, P + parking brake.

    Scoring: start with 100 points, pass with 70+. Typical demerits: no signal −10, late signal −5, safety check missed −10, kerb −20, pole −20, harsh braking −10. 試験中止 (instant fail): running a stop line, level crossing or red light, rolling back ≥ 1 m. Values are approximate — they follow the usual 減点 table.
